* Purpose
Formal, highly cited references on optical fibre splitters -- how light is shared between
guides, and how 1xN splitters are made (fused biconical taper, planar lightwave circuit,
multimode interference, star couplers) -- in place of the vendor overview first read:
[[https://www.weunionfiber.com/how-does-a-fiber-optic-splitter-work-a-comprehensive-guide/][How does a fiber optic splitter work? (WeUnion Fiber)]].

Entries tagged =:library:= are already in [[file:~/Scramble Private/References/][Scramble Private / References]] (linked).

* Suggested reading order
1. Okamoto, /Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides/ -- coupled-mode theory, then the planar
   lightwave circuit chapters (directional couplers, Y-branches, star couplers, AWGs).
2. Love et al. (1991), Part 1 -- the adiabaticity criterion behind fused (FBT) splitters.
3. Himeno, Kato, Miya (1998) -- how silica PLC splitters are designed and made.
4. Soldano & Pennings (1995) -- multimode-interference (MMI) splitters.
5. Lam (2007) -- where 1xN splitters are used, and how their datasheets specify them.

* Books
** "Optical Waveguide Theory"                                        :library:
- Authors :: A. W. Snyder, J. D. Love
- Published :: Chapman & Hall, London, 1983
- Why :: The classic: modes, coupled power, tapers and couplers worked through in full.
- File :: [[file:~/Scramble Private/References/Optical Waveguide Theory.pdf][Optical Waveguide Theory.pdf]]
** "Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides"                              :library:
- Authors :: K. Okamoto
- Published :: Academic Press (Elsevier), 2nd ed. 2006
- Why :: The best single book to start with: coupled-mode theory, directional couplers, star
  couplers, planar lightwave circuit splitters and arrayed-waveguide gratings.
- File :: [[file:~/Scramble Private/References/Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides.pdf][Fundamentals of Optical Waveguides.pdf]]
** "Theory of Dielectric Optical Waveguides"                         :library:
- Authors :: D. Marcuse
- Published :: Academic Press, 2nd ed. 1991
- Why :: Coupled-mode theory and losses of joints and bends, by the author of the splice-loss
  formulas used throughout trident's fibre work.
- File :: [[file:~/Scramble Private/References/Theory of dielectric optical waveguides.pdf][Theory of dielectric optical waveguides.pdf]]
** "Integrated Optics: Theory and Technology"                        :library:
- Authors :: R. G. Hunsperger
- Published :: Springer (several editions)
- Why :: Its coupler and integrated-component chapters: a gentler route into planar devices.
- File :: [[file:~/Scramble Private/References/Integrated Optics - Theory and Technology.pdf][Integrated Optics - Theory and Technology.pdf]]
** "Guided Optics: Optical Fibers and All-fiber Components"
- Authors :: J. Bures
- Published :: Wiley-VCH, 2009
- Why :: From the Montreal group behind practical fused biconical taper couplers: fused
  couplers and tapers in depth.
** "Silica-based Buried Channel Waveguides and Devices"
- Authors :: F. Ladouceur, J. D. Love
- Published :: Chapman & Hall, 1996
- Why :: Y-junction splitters and buried silica channel waveguides, design to loss.
** "Passive Optical Networks: Principles and Practice"
- Authors :: C. F. Lam (ed.)
- Published :: Academic Press, 2007
- Why :: System context: where 1xN splitters sit in PON, and their specifications --
  insertion loss, uniformity, polarisation-dependent loss.

* Journals
** Foundations
*** "Coupled-mode theory for optical waveguides: an overview"
- Authors :: W.-P. Huang
- Published :: J. Opt. Soc. Am. A 11(3), 963-983, 1994
- Why :: The theory every directional-coupler splitter is designed with, reviewed.
** Fused biconical taper (FBT) splitters
*** "Biconical-taper single-mode fiber coupler"
- Authors :: B. S. Kawasaki, K. O. Hill, R. G. Lamont
- Published :: Optics Letters 6(7), 327-328, 1981
- Why :: The origin of the fused biconical taper coupler.
*** "Tapered single-mode fibres and devices. Part 1: Adiabaticity criteria"
- Authors :: J. D. Love, W. M. Henry, W. J. Stewart, R. J. Black, S. Lacroix, F. Gonthier
- Published :: IEE Proceedings J (Optoelectronics) 138(5), 1991 -- page range given
  differently by different sources (343-354 most often): check before citing
- Why :: The adiabaticity (delineation) criterion: how gently a taper must change for the
  fundamental mode to survive -- the design rule of fused splitters. Highly cited.
- Companion :: "Part 2: Experimental and theoretical quantification", R. J. Black,
  S. Lacroix, F. Gonthier, J. D. Love, same issue.
*** "The shape of fiber tapers"
- Authors :: T. A. Birks, Y. W. Li
- Published :: J. Lightwave Technol. 10(4), 432-438, 1992
- Why :: How the heat-and-pull process sets the taper's profile.
** Planar lightwave circuit (PLC) splitters
*** "Silica waveguides on silicon and their application to integrated-optic components"
- Authors :: M. Kawachi
- Published :: Optical and Quantum Electronics 22, 391-416, 1990
- Why :: The foundation of silica-on-silicon planar circuits, from which PLC splitters come.
*** "Silica-based planar lightwave circuits"
- Authors :: A. Himeno, K. Kato, T. Miya
- Published :: IEEE J. Sel. Top. Quantum Electron. 4(6), 913-924, 1998
- Why :: The standard review: power splitters, AWGs, thermo-optic switches, hybrid
  integration -- how 1xN PLC chips are made.
*** "Mode conversion in planar-dielectric separating waveguides"
- Authors :: W. K. Burns, A. F. Milton
- Published :: IEEE J. Quantum Electron. 11(1), 32-39, 1975
- Why :: The theory of the adiabatic Y-branch, the building block of cascaded 1xN splitters.
** Multimode interference (MMI) and star couplers
*** "Optical multi-mode interference devices based on self-imaging: principles and applications"
- Authors :: L. B. Soldano, E. C. M. Pennings
- Published :: J. Lightwave Technol. 13(4), 615-627, 1995
- Why :: One of the most cited papers in integrated optics: MMI 1xN splitters by
  self-imaging.
*** "Efficient NxN star couplers using Fourier optics"
- Authors :: C. Dragone
- Published :: J. Lightwave Technol. 7(3), 479-489, 1989
- Why :: The free-propagation-region star coupler: large N in one region, and the heart of
  the AWG.
** On-chip silicon photonics splitters
*** "A compact and low loss Y-junction for submicron silicon waveguide"
- Authors :: Y. Zhang et al.
- Published :: Optics Express 21(1), 1310-1316, 2013
- Why :: The optimised silicon Y-junction -- relevant to splitting on a silicon chip such
  as the device's.
